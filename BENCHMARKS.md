# Benchmarks and Datasets for Scientific Literature Retrieval

A dataset-level companion to the [Benchmarks & Test Collections](README.md#benchmarks--test-collections) section of the README. The README lists papers; this file lists the **datasets** inside them, one row per dataset or subtask, because a single benchmark often bundles several datasets that are built and scored differently. ScholarQABench, for example, holds seven datasets, and only one of them comes with grading rubrics.

It also covers datasets released inside method papers (ScholarQABench in OpenScholar, LitQA2 in PaperQA2, RealScholarQuery in PaSa, and others), and a few widely reused datasets that have no entry of their own in the list (ACL-200, RefSeer, TripClick). The groups follow the README's benchmark subsections.

## How to read the columns

- **Size** is the number of evaluation queries (questions, claims, citation contexts) unless stated otherwise; training sets are given in parentheses where they exist.
- **Corpus** is what systems search: its source, size, and retrieval unit. *Live* means systems search the open web or a search API instead of a fixed collection, so results depend on when the run was made.
- **Queries from** is who or what wrote the queries.
- **Gold from** is who or what decided the correct answers: experts, the papers' own authors, citation edges, user clicks, or an LLM judge.
- **Gold form** is what the correct answer looks like:
  - a set of relevant papers (binary or graded)
  - a single target paper
  - a label
  - a reference answer or rubric
  - none, when only citation quality is scored, or when relevance is held fixed by counterfactual edits

  Benchmarks with a reference answer or rubric judge the final answer and its citations, not a ranked list, so their numbers are not comparable with recall on a test collection.
- **Notes** carries the date cutoff or leakage controls where the paper states them, and one headline result.

Numbers come from each dataset's own paper, preferring its tables over its prose; where they disagree, the table is used and the discrepancy is noted. Counts for ACL-200, RefSeer and arXiv (HAtten) are as tabulated in SymTax, and TREC-COVID's final size is from BEIR.
